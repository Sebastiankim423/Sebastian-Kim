# Research Brief

## Challenge
The collapse of the traditional television broadcasting model in professional basketball. 

## Why It Matters
Regional Sports Networks (RSNs) have historically provided NBA teams with local media revenue. As consumers continue to move away from cable television and adopt streaming services, RSNs are losing subscribers and revenue.

## Economic Concepts
1. Supply and Demand
2. Elasticity
3. Consumer Choice
4. Market Structure and Pricing Power

## Planned Analysis
I will analyze how cord-cutting affects NBA teams, regional sports networks, and consumers. I will evaluate the economic implications and recommend a strategy for the NBA moving forward.
