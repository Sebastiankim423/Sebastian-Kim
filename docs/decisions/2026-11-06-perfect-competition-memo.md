# Perfect Competition Recommendation Memo

## Judgement Call
The carrot and mesclun bed caps were tested by increasing each cap by one additional bed and re-running Solver.

Neither change increased profit or altered the optimal solution.

Under current conditions, neither constraint provides enough additional value to justify expansion. Additional beds should not be purchased unless crop prices, labor costs, or production economics change.

## Recommendation

Plant: 
- 10 Tomato Beds
- 20 Carrot Beds
- 30 Mesclun Beds
## Why

This crop mix produced the highest season profit while meeting all labor and bed constraints.

## Main Constraint 
The most important constraints were the crop bed caps, especially for carrots and mesclun.

## What Would Change my Recommendation 
A significant change in crop prices, labor costs, fertilizer costs, or the tomato marginal-cost schedule would change the recommended planting mix.
